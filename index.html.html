About Gill Exotic Palms

At Gill Exotic Palms our passion is palms. Our staff has been cultivating and caring for beautiful healthy palms for decades. We have a wide variety of both indoor and outdoor palms, from small specimen size palms to large exotic trees. All our palms are grown and tended to with loving care. We believe that your home or business should have a touch of the tropics.
